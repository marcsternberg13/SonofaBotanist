Who We Are

Son of a Botanist Productions

An independent production company documenting communities that force industries to change. Every project on our slate is built around a single framework: communities historically shut out of dominant cultural narratives, and how they use excellence and market forces to reclaim their place.

Creative Producer Marc Sternberg has 12+ years of producing experience, a slate of 10+ properties spanning documentary features, docuseries, and short films — and 20+ years of global marketing leadership that provides the financier credibility and operational discipline to get projects made.

The Art and Life of Poteet Victory

Poteet Victory is Cherokee-Choctaw, born in Idabel, Oklahoma — raised ninety miles from the end of the Trail of Trails. He built a T-shirt empire, taught Andy Warhol silk screening, arrived in Santa Fe with nothing, and became the most in-demand Indigenous fine artist alive. He has been called the "Forrest Gump of the art world."

About

Marc Sternberg,
MA, MBA, MFA

Creative Producer · Fulbright Specialist · Entrepreneur

I've been making films since I was eight years old. My parents steered me toward business school instead of film school, so I pursued an MBA, built and sold a company, and spent twenty years leading global marketing operations. Filmmaking never left me. I came back to it with the resources, discipline, and perspective that only that kind of detour can give you.
